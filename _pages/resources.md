## Other Recources

### Development

* [Salem Wasteland "Creating Your First Voicebank" Tutorial](https://utau.felinewasteland.com/ref/firstvb) - This is the guide I recommend to people who want to make their own UTAU voicebanks! You make a very simplistic Japanese voicebank (Starts with just CV, then adds VC phonemes later), but it builds a lot of the skills that you use in more complex voicebanks as well! I used it to make [Suigin Koora CVVC V1](/singers/suigin-koora/suigin-koora-ja-cvvc-v1/).
* [vLabeler](https://vlabeler.com/) - This is what I use to OTO as well as label DiffSigner data. I haven't tried any alternatives.
* [RecStar](https://github.com/sdercolin/recstar) - This is what I use when recording for UTAU and DeepVocal. It is a lot simpler to use than OREMO in my opinion. I use it on my pc, but I used it once on my phone (when recording with my teacher for [Haruka](/singers/haruka/))
* [UtaUtaUtau's NNSVS Labeling Scripts](https://github.com/UtaUtaUtau/nnsvslabeling) - This has both `threaded_noise_remove.py` (and `.exe`) as well as `harvest_frq.exe`, which I use when making voicebanks. There's also a bunch of other stuff that I haven't used.

### Usage

* [UtauV](https://github.com/emeraldsingers/UtauV) - My personal favorite fork of OpenUtau. It has lots of extra features!
* [OpenUtau](https://www.openutau.com/) - The main version of OpenUtau (more online support than UtauV (Discords, etc.), but less fancy features)
* [REAPER](https://www.reaper.fm/) - The DAW that I use for mixing. I'm still not very good at mixing, but I'm learning :)
