This is a voicebank that I made with a friend. 
Since it was the second voicebank I recorded (first after mine), it is lower quality than some of my other ones, but I think it sounds good. 
It has a very calm tone.

## Character Info:

- Name
  - Romaji/English: Banjo
  - Kana: ばんじょお
  - Kanji: バンジョー

# Voicebank Info:

## JA CVVC

Demo Song: Haruni (Nilfruits)

*This is one of the first songs I ever made so it is very low quality (I hadn't heard the term "mixing" before) but you can hear the voicebank at some parts.*

### Info

- Reclist: [Salem Wasteland JA CVVC 8-Mora](https://wastelandutau.neocities.org/jp/cvvc#8)
- Recorded Pitch: A2 @ 120 BPM (Optimum Pitch Range: E2~G4)
- Extra Features:
  - Standalone consonant phonemes (C -):
  - Usage example: [で][e s][s -] [か][a -]
