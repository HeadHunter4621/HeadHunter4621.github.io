Goals and stuff for 2026

## Since it's 2026 now, I want to share some of my goals with you all.

- I want to release all of [Suigin Koora's](/singers/suigin-koora/) voicebanks, including [the DeepVocal](/singers/suigin-koora/ja-dv-v1/) (once I make it better).
- I want to expand my YouTube channel, mainly by posting YT Shorts when I upload new videos, and to give previews of my new voicebanks (I have already uploaded quite a few!)

## Also, some small updates:

If you look at Suigin Koora's page, you'll see that they now have a DeepVocal voicebank. I ported the CVVC 2.0 recordings to it and reconfigured it entirely. It still needs a lot of work of course, but it is cool to see how it sounds. The configuration process felt much more professional than UTAU/vLabeler's, I'm not really sure how to explain that, but it feels like it was meant to be used to make commercial voicebanks and not just hobbyist projects.

As you may have noticed, the entire structure of the website has changed a bit, and I think that is a very, very good thing. Voicebanks specific to characters (for example, [Suigin Koora VCV V1](/singers/suigin-koora/vcv-v1)) now has its own page, seperate from [Suigin Koora's](/singers/suigin-koora)!
