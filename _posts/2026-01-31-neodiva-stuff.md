NeoDiva released OIL and Suigin Koora DS!

Last evening I forgot to make an update about this, but NeoDiva released the DiffSinger mode for both OIL and Suigin Koora! You can download them from [NeoDiva's website](https://neodiva.xyz)! Their singer pages are now updated.

Additionally, I am working on a new JA CVVC voicebank for Suigin Koora. It has more voice colors (regular, power, whisper, and NT (recorded while plugging my nose)) as well as covering a total of 3 pitches. Eventually there will be a chart with all of them.
