Testing stuff and finding how to make things sound better

I tested UtaUtaUtau's [Threaded Noise Removal Python script](https://github.com/UtaUtaUtau/nnsvslabeling/blob/main/threaded_noise_remove.py) on En'ne Jeho's voicebank (since that was the only one I had installed on my laptop). The difference wasn't very noticeable when you listen to the audio with this voicebank since it has very little background noise, but some of my newer ones (Suigin Claire, Ely, Oil, etc.) have quite a lot. I am going to run this script on En'ne Jeho as well as all of my other voicebanks because I think it will make them more consistent. It should also help some of them sound better.
