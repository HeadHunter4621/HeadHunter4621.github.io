---
title: "Suigin Koora's VCVX Voicebank is Released"
---

You can now download and use [Suigin Koora's VCVX voicebank](/singers/suigin-koora/ja-vcvx/)! Right now, it has all VCV phonemes (and the CVVC phonemes for the extra consonants). It is usable with the Japanese presamp phonemizer, but I would recommend you download and use mt [CVVX phonemizer](/resources/cvvx-info), which will allow you to use all of the voicebank's features. CV and VC phonemes will be added in a future update, soon.
