I'm working on a reclist/oto/voicebank for [Lisatopian](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lcsluv17qNU), a conlang by [L4MPLIGHT](https://www.youtube.com/@L4MPLIGHT)

It's a really fun project. The phonetics are really simple (with a few exceptins), since it's CV with the same consonants as toki pona (other than w being replaced with v) and an ending consonant s
I originally made it CVVC, but after placing the phonemes manually (very time consuming!!), I think I am going to try to make it VCV instead, with VsCV phonemes when there is the s coda (and a VC [V s] phoneme for the end of words)

I think I will release it once it's done, but it will take a while lol
